We build brands too. So we know the problems.

We run brands of our own, and for years we have also made products for other brands. Building our own taught us where brand owners get stuck with a factory.

6 questions · 6 answers
  • Q.01

    “My order is too small for them.”

    Every project follows the same clear process.

  • Q.02

    “You have your own brands. Will you copy my formula?”

    NDA on request, ownership written in your contract.

  • Q.03

    “I waited months, and the date was late.”

    Clear stages from brief to batch release.

  • Q.04

    “Will the next batch match the first?”

    Each batch is released with a certificate of analysis.

  • Q.05

    “I don’t know what it will cost or how long it will take.”

    Timelines and the best path before you commit.

  • Q.06

    “Paperwork stops me from selling abroad.”

    We support the documents your export needs.

  1. 01Today
    Where brand owners get stuck

    A brand owner hands their idea, formula and launch date to a factory that sees one more order.

    Small orders wait. Dates move. Batches change. Costs stay unclear.

  2. 02Mission
    What we do

    We develop, make and document cosmetics for brand owners.

    With clear stages and the same care we give our own brands.

  3. 03Method
    How we work

    Our lab does the work. You see every stage.

    Our own R&D team develops and tests your product. Stages, timelines and documents come before you commit.

  4. 04Vision
    Where we are going

    A cosmetics industry where a brand owner of any size knows what is made, when, and who owns it.

  5. 05Purpose
    Why it matters

    Help brand owners launch and grow without losing control of their product.
